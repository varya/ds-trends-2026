Context

Why this year is different

The conversation has shifted from building design systems to operating them.

Tokens are no longer the frontier — adoption is.

AI tooling has reached the design system itself, not just the artifacts it produces.

Teams are measuring outcomes, not component counts.

Shift in stance

What teams stopped doing — and started

Then · 2022–2024

  • Counting components shipped
  • Annual rebrand sprints
  • Figma library as source of truth
  • Adoption assumed, not measured

Now · 2026

  • Counting components adopted
  • Continuous token-driven theming
  • Code as source of truth, Figma as mirror
  • Adoption dashboards in every team review
ds-trends-202603 / 06

Deep dive · 01

AI is now operating inside the design system

Last cycle, AI generated screens. This cycle, it manages the system that screens come from — review, lint, codemod, and choice.

  • Component selection prompts trained on the team’s own usage data
  • Linting that explains why a token violates intent, not just that it does
  • Codemods generated from a Figma diff and a sentence of context
  • Eval suites for AI-authored UI that gate merges
ds-trends-202604 / 06

Deep dive · 02

Adoption is the metric that finally stuck

When DS teams started reporting adoption rates the way growth teams report MAU, budget conversations changed.

  • Per-team adoption ratio surfaced in quarterly reviews
  • Drift detection runs on every PR — not in monthly audits
  • Migration debt is tracked as a first-class engineering metric
  • “Components used” is reported alongside “components shipped”
