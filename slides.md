<p class="eyebrow">Context</p>

## Why this year is different

<p class="statement">The conversation has shifted from <em>building</em> design systems to <em>operating</em> them.</p>

<div class="statement-details">
  <p>Tokens are no longer the frontier &mdash; adoption is.</p>
  <p>AI tooling has reached the design system itself, not just the artifacts it produces.</p>
  <p>Teams are measuring outcomes, not component counts.</p>
</div>

<p class="eyebrow">The shortlist</p>

## Six trends to watch

<div class="cards">
  <div class="card">
    <span class="num">01</span>
    <h3>AI-native components</h3>
    <p>Components that ship with prompts, eval suites, and behavioral contracts.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card b">
    <span class="num">02</span>
    <h3>Token pipelines mature</h3>
    <p>DTCG settles in. Tokens flow from Figma to platform binaries without glue code.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card r">
    <span class="num">03</span>
    <h3>Adoption as a product</h3>
    <p>DS teams measure usage, drift, and migration debt the way SaaS teams measure MAU.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <span class="num">04</span>
    <h3>Accessibility on the diff</h3>
    <p>WCAG checks land in PR review, not in audits months later.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card b">
    <span class="num">05</span>
    <h3>Headless &amp; styled, reunited</h3>
    <p>The split between primitives and styled kits narrows as wrappers consolidate.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card r">
    <span class="num">06</span>
    <h3>Governance via codemods</h3>
    <p>Migrations become automated changes, not Slack announcements.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="eyebrow">Shift in stance</p>

## What teams stopped doing &mdash; and started

<div class="compare">
  <div class="then">
    <h3>Then &middot; 2022&ndash;2024</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Counting components shipped</li>
      <li>Annual rebrand sprints</li>
      <li>Figma library as source of truth</li>
      <li>Adoption assumed, not measured</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="now">
    <h3>Now &middot; 2026</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Counting components <em>adopted</em></li>
      <li>Continuous token-driven theming</li>
      <li>Code as source of truth, Figma as mirror</li>
      <li>Adoption dashboards in every team review</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="eyebrow">Deep dive &middot; 01</p>

## AI is now operating <em>inside</em> the design system

<p class="lede">Last cycle, AI generated screens. This cycle, it manages the system that screens come from &mdash; review, lint, codemod, and choice.</p>

<ul class="dotted">
  <li>Component selection prompts trained on the team&rsquo;s own usage data</li>
  <li>Linting that explains <em>why</em> a token violates intent, not just that it does</li>
  <li>Codemods generated from a Figma diff and a sentence of context</li>
  <li>Eval suites for AI-authored UI that gate merges</li>
</ul>

<div class="footer"><span class="url">ds-trends-2026</span><span class="mark">04 / 06</span></div>

<p class="eyebrow">Deep dive &middot; 02</p>

## Adoption is the metric that finally stuck

<p class="lede">When DS teams started reporting adoption rates the way growth teams report MAU, budget conversations changed.</p>

<ul class="dotted">
  <li>Per-team adoption ratio surfaced in quarterly reviews</li>
  <li>Drift detection runs on every PR &mdash; not in monthly audits</li>
  <li>Migration debt is tracked as a first-class engineering metric</li>
  <li>&ldquo;Components used&rdquo; is reported alongside &ldquo;components shipped&rdquo;</li>
</ul>
